Independent Portfolio Project · Completed Jun 2025

12.47 kV Residential Distribution Pole Design

Designed and analyzed a utility-style overhead distribution structure end-to-end: structural loading, guying analysis, grounding design, and NESC Grade C construction documentation for a 35-ft Class 5 wood pole in a double dead-end configuration.

Final ground resistance
Engineering Decision

Initial single-rod grounding calculated at 42 Ω, exceeding the 25 Ω project requirement. Rather than switch to lower-resistivity backfill, I specified two bonded 5/8"×8' copper-clad rods spaced 16 ft apart — bringing resistance to 21 Ω while keeping the design on standard utility hardware.
