What the chatbot does

  • Answers questions instantly from your own website: services, prices, hours, booking and service area.
  • Captures a visitor's name, phone number and what they need, and sends it to you as a lead.
  • Works after hours, on weekends and on holidays, when nobody is at the desk.
  • Points visitors to your booking page or takes a callback request.
  • Lives on your website and on mobile, no app to install.
